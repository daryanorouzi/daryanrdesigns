Visual Identity

Mehregan

A contemporary identity inspired by the warmth, symbolism, and atmosphere of the Mehregan festival.

This identity system reinterprets traditional cultural references through a contemporary visual language. The project balances minimal typography, color, and composition to communicate celebration, connection, and atmosphere without relying on decorative excess. The visual direction focuses on clarity and emotional resonance while preserving the essence of the festival’s cultural roots.
